what_i_learned: |
 Today, I attended a guest lecture by Chris S. Crawford. He talked about the exciting research happening in the Human Technology Interaction Lab. I learned how machine learning can be used to help computers understand and respond to human actions. We did two hands-on activities using biosignals. In the first activity, we used ECG (Electrocardiography) and other sensors to control a drone with facial and head movements, like blinking or tilting the head. In the second activity, we used EMG (Electromyography) signals from arm muscles to move a small robot. It was amazing to see how human body signals can be used to control machines through technology. After that we worked on our research paper and powerpoint slides for final symposium.
